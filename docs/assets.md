# Assets

Everything in `public/assets/models/` and `public/assets/textures/` is generated by scripts in `tools/`; `public/assets/hdri/` holds one downloaded CC0 photograph.
Coordinates are machine coordinates: x right, y up, z toward the typist, metres, origin on the desk under
the machine centre. No text, logos or manufacturer names appear in any asset or prompt.

## Licence caveat (check before any commercial use)
The pinstripe decal comes from **Qwen-Image 2.1**. 

## Frame: `public/assets/models/frame.glb`
A procedural black japanned open frame, 41 k triangles, 1.5 MB, metres, Y-up glTF. Node transforms are all
identity, so vertices are already in machine coordinates.

| Node | Contents |
|---|---|
| `frame_base` | Base plate with moulded rib and open centre, key-lever fulcrum cross rib, arched front apron, four rubber feet |
| `frame_sides` | Two windowed side plates with raised casting borders, ribbon-spool brackets, nickel bolts |
| `frame_rails` | Two nickel carriage rails, rail saddles, turned rear uprights, low rear tie |
| `segment` | Typebar segment casting: slotted comb (one tooth between each pair of bars), rib, arched girder and shoulder arms to the side plates |

Materials (glTF metallic-roughness factors, no embedded images):

| Material | Used for | Base / metallic / roughness |
|---|---|---|
| `japanned` | all cast iron | near-black / 0 / 0.22 |
| `nickel` | rails, bolts | 0.72, 0.70, 0.66 / 1 / 0.32 |
| `rubber` | feet | 0.02 / 0 / 0.85 |

**UVs** are box-projected from the surface in metres, divided by 0.2 (`UV_METRES` in `build_frame.py`): one UV
unit covers 0.2 m of surface on every face. There is a UV seam wherever the dominant face axis changes, so use
textures that tolerate it (all of those below do). Normal maps use the OpenGL convention (+G up).

### Regenerate
```sh
node tools/gen/export_machine.mjs                                  # machine.json from the definition and rig
~/bin/blender --background --python tools/blender/build_frame.py   # frame.glb, then the clearance check
~/bin/blender --background --python tools/blender/render_previews.py -- [--fit] [--samples N] [--size N] [--views hero,front,side,top,rear,segment]
```
Run the first two after any change to the machine definition or `src/render/rig.js`. `build_frame.py` exits 1 if
the frame intersects a moving part.

### Clearances (`tools/blender/check_clearance.py`, report in `tools/blender/clearance_report.json`)
Each moving part is sampled as capsules over its whole motion and tested against each node with a BVH (nearest
surface, with a normal and ray-parity inside test). Run it on its own under Blender with
`-- public/assets/models/frame.glb` to re-check the exported file rather than the build scene.

Checked: keys, caps, stems, levers (straight and fanned to the centre fulcrum), the six control keys, every
typebar and slug in 1.5 degree steps through its whole arc, platen and knobs over the carriage travel taken
symmetric about centre (x to about +-0.30 m) with shift in both directions, full ribbon spools, and the
renderer's rack, carriage beams, universal bar, rocker and bell (`rig.js`).

The check also covers the paper table and paper path, ribbon path (rest and full lift), line-space, carriage-release
and paper-release levers and bail through their swing, at both ends and the middle of the carriage travel.

The tightest clearances are by design: comb teeth to typebars 0.23 mm (measured from the bar's half-diagonal
capsule, so the real bar section has about 0.5 mm per side at the widest), rack mount to the rear rail saddle 0.25 mm,
universal bar to segment 0.25 mm. Everything else is 2 mm or more.

Not checked, because the definition does not place them: sublevers, type guide, line-space lever swing, carriage
end plates beyond the sampled knobs. The frame has no fulcrum wire, type guide, key comb or felt rest pad; the
renderer's placeholder drew some of these and would need to keep them or add its own.

## Enclosure: `public/assets/models/enclosure.glb`
A formed sheet-steel shell, 47.8 k tris, 1.2 mm sheet with real thickness, all nodes identity, in machine coordinates.
Nodes (remove any of them independently):

| Node | Contents |
|---|---|
| `enclosure_side_left`, `enclosure_side_right` | Skirts rolling in at the shoulder, with a pressed belly and belt line, front and rear corners, and a bright trim strip |
| `enclosure_deck_left`, `enclosure_deck_right` | Shoulder decks beside the keyboard and type basket, with a rolled edge |
| `enclosure_front` | Low front panel under the space bar, arched, with trim |
| `enclosure_rear` | Rear panel, scooped low in the middle to show the escapement and rack |

Materials: `enclosure_paint` (use `powder_coat`, or `wrinkle_paint`, below) and `enclosure_trim` (`chrome_steel` or
`nickel_polished`). UVs as for the frame (0.2 m per unit). The keyboard, the type basket, the spools and the whole
carriage are in the open: the deck cannot cross between the basket and the platen, because the typebars' fan and the
carriage sweep occupy that space.

```sh
~/bin/blender --background --python tools/blender/build_enclosure.py
```
exits 1 on any intersection and writes `tools/blender/clearance_enclosure.json`. Minimum clearances, mm (bars through
the whole arc, keys at full depression, carriage through its full travel with shift, levers, paper and ribbon):

| Moving part | Clearance |
|---|---|
| Rack and rack mount | 2.20 |
| Controls (space bar) | 3.78 |
| Carriage beams | 4.35 |
| Platen knobs | 5.14 |
| Stems and levers | 9.8 and more |
| Typebars / slugs | 14.6 / 10.8 |
| Paper path / table | 14.8 / 20.9 |
| Key caps | 25.1 |
| Ribbon spools | 31.8 |
| Static gap, shell to frame | 0.82 (rail ends) |

The paper's soft body does not collide with the shell: a sheet flopped over onto the deck will pass through it.

## Materials: `public/assets/textures/`
Authored procedurally by `tools/gen/materials.py`. Heights are in metres at the tile size, so normals are true slopes and `normalScale` 1 is correct.
Metals hold their measured F0 in albedo. `materials.json` holds the tile sizes and physical parameters for code to read.

Files per set: `_albedo` (sRGB), `_normal` (linear, +G up), `_roughness`, `_metalness` (grey, three reads G and B of
them), and `_roughness_mips` (below). 2048 px, seamless.

| Set | Tile T (m) | `MeshPhysicalMaterial` beyond the maps | Use / notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| `powder_coat` | 0.08 | `clearcoat 0.2, clearcoatRoughness 0.42, ior 1.5` | enclosure (default). Orange peel + 2.8 % metallic flake |
| `wrinkle_paint` | 0.06 | `ior 1.5` | enclosure alternative. 1.35 mm labyrinth |
| `bead_blasted_aluminium` | 0.04 | — | satin bare aluminium; F0 (0.913, 0.922, 0.924) |
| `nickel_polished` | 0.06 | `envMapIntensity 1.1` | bright plate; F0 (0.660, 0.609, 0.526) |
| `nickel_worn` | 0.10 | — | rubbed through to brass, then steel; corrosion pits are dielectric. Also served as `nickel_plating_*` |
| `chrome_steel` | 0.06 | `envMapIntensity 1.15` | trim; F0 (0.549, 0.556, 0.554) |
| `key_glass` | 0.03 | `clearcoat 1, clearcoatRoughness 0.02, ior 1.52` | key caps; multiply the legend over the card |
| `blued_spring_steel` | 0.05 | `anisotropy 0.55` | springs; rolling lines run along U |
| `japanned_enamel` | 0.20 | `clearcoat 1, clearcoatRoughness 0.07, ior 1.52` | the casting; chips show metalness |
| `vulcanised_rubber` | 0.10 | — | feet, platen |
| `green_felt` | 0.08 | `sheen 0.35, sheenRoughness 0.65, sheenColor (0.1,0.26,0.13)` | felt |
| `paper_fibre` | 0.2159 | — | sheet |
| `ribbon_cloth` | 0.0248 | `sheen 0.3, sheenRoughness 0.5` | 40 threads at the definition's pitch |
| `walnut_desk` | 2.2 | dielectric, `clearcoat` 0.45, `clearcoatRoughness` 0.2 | French-polished walnut boards, 4096 px (0.54 mm/texel), grain along U, one tile covers the desk so it never repeats |

Set `metalness: 1` and `roughness: 1` whenever a map drives them (three multiplies), and leave `material.color` white so
measured F0 and base colour are not altered. `repeat = uvMetresPerUnit / T`: for `frame.glb` and `enclosure.glb`
that is `0.2 / T`.

`pinstripe_border.png` is a separate 2048 x 2048 RGBA decal (a gold line border with scrolled corners).

### Specular aliasing
Each set's normal variance is folded into roughness at source (Toksvig: variance from the shortened mean normal):
`_roughness.jpg` carries this at mip 0, and `_roughness_mips.jpg` is the full filtered chain, so glints widen and dim
instead of shimmering with distance. Keep `normalScale` at or below 1. To use the chain, slice the atlas (L0 is
`[0, 0, H, H]`, then L1, L2, ... stacked at `x = H`):
```js
const atlas = await createImageBitmap(blob);                   // <name>_roughness_mips.jpg
const H = atlas.height, mips = [await createImageBitmap(atlas, 0, 0, H, H, { imageOrientation: 'flipY' })];
for (let s = H >> 1, y = 0; s >= 1; y += s, s >>= 1) mips.push(await createImageBitmap(atlas, H, y, s, s, { imageOrientation: 'flipY' }));
const tex = new THREE.Texture(mips[0]);
tex.mipmaps = mips; tex.generateMipmaps = false; tex.minFilter = THREE.LinearMipmapLinearFilter; tex.flipY = false;
tex.colorSpace = THREE.NoColorSpace; tex.wrapS = tex.wrapT = THREE.RepeatWrapping; tex.anisotropy = 16; tex.needsUpdate = true;
```


## HDRI: `public/assets/hdri/combination_room_2k.hdr`
| | |
|---|---|
| Asset | Combination Room, 2048 x 1024 Radiance HDR, 6.5 MB, md5 `1f43bc52010775159f5c4270c68596b9` |
| Source | https://polyhaven.com/a/combination_room, file `https://dl.polyhaven.org/file/ph-assets/HDRIs/hdr/2k/combination_room_2k.hdr` |
| Author | Sergej Majboroda |
| Licence | CC0 1.0 (public domain dedication, no attribution required), stated on the asset's page; https://polyhaven.com/license |
| Checked | 2026-11-08 |

A Victorian parlour with curtains and a large window. Measured in Blender: peak luminance 94, 99.9th percentile 32,
no sun disc; the window holds about 37 % of the upper hemisphere's energy. In the file the window is at azimuth
atan2(z, x) = +45 degrees.

The one file does both jobs. A sharper backdrop was not needed: at a 29 degree lens the photograph is magnified so
far that a sharp copy would look wrong anyway, and blurred it reads as depth of field. See the settings below.

### Renderer settings
```js
const tex = await new RGBELoader().loadAsync('/assets/hdri/combination_room_2k.hdr');
tex.mapping = THREE.EquirectangularReflectionMapping;
scene.environment = tex;  scene.environmentIntensity = 0.6;   scene.environmentRotation.set(0, 3.0, 0);
scene.background  = tex;  scene.backgroundIntensity  = 0.45;  scene.backgroundBlurriness = 0.06;  scene.backgroundRotation.set(0, 3.0, 0);
key.intensity = 3.2;      // from 4.6; rim unchanged. Remove the floor and the backdrop dome.
```
Rotation moves a feature at azimuth `a` to `a - rotation`, so 3.0 rad places the window at about -127 degrees, directly
behind the machine from the default camera. For the window at the typist's left instead, matching the key light, use
-1.83 rad; that puts the wallpaper behind the machine, which is busier.

## Desk: `public/assets/models/desk.glb`
`tools/blender/build_desk.py`, 308 triangles, one node `desk`, one material `desk_wood`, 2.0 x 1.2 m, x -1..1,
z -0.56..0.64, 0.74 m high. The top surface is exactly y = 0 and nothing rises above it; the script exits 1 if that
fails, or if `frame.glb` or `enclosure.glb` goes below the top or overhangs the boards (frame lowest y 0.0000, feet
on the surface; enclosure lowest y 0.0033).

A 40 mm top: boards running along x, framed by a mitred band with a thumbnail moulding, quirk and cove, then a recessed
apron and four tapered legs. UVs are authored rather than box projected (one unit = 0.2 m, as elsewhere, so
`repeat = 0.2 / 2.2`), with the grain along U everywhere; it follows each length of moulding and turns at the mitres,
and runs down the legs.

The board field is 1.9 x 1.1 m (x -0.948..0.948, z -0.508..0.588), with room for a sheet of paper on either side of
the machine.

### Camera limits
The far edge of the desk is meant to be seen, against the room. To keep the near and side edges out of frame set
`rMax` about 1.2 (now 2.2) and `phiMax` about 1.45 (now 1.54) in `src/interact/orbit.js`, checked from the default
azimuth only. Past that the whole table shows, standing on nothing: the legs end at y = -0.74 with no floor under them.
